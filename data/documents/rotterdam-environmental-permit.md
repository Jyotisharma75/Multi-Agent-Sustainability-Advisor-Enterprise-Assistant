# Scope of the permit

<!-- page: 1 -->

This summary lists the environmental permit conditions for the Rotterdam Polymer Plant (FAC-001) that relate to energy and greenhouse gas emissions. The full permit is held by the site environmental manager.

# Combustion installations

<!-- page: 3 -->

The steam boiler house is a combustion installation subject to monitoring. The operator must continuously monitor natural gas consumption of the boilers and keep hourly records for at least five years. Any deviation of more than 25 percent from the expected monthly fuel use must be investigated and recorded within 30 days, including the root cause and corrective action.

# Energy efficiency obligation

<!-- page: 5 -->

The operator must carry out an energy efficiency audit every four years and implement all energy saving measures with a payback period of five years or less. The audit report must be submitted to the competent authority. Heat recovery on the boiler flue gas was identified in the last audit as a measure to be assessed.

# Reporting

<!-- page: 6 -->

The operator must submit an annual environmental report to the competent authority by 1 April each year, covering energy consumption, CO2 emissions from combustion and any incidents or deviations recorded during the year.
