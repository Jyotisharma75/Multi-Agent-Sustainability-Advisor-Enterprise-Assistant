# Summary

<!-- page: 1 -->

The energy audit of the Monterrey Assembly Site (FAC-002) reviewed electricity and gas use across the stamping, machining and assembly lines. Electricity accounts for most of site energy use. Compressed air is the largest single electrical end use.

# Findings

<!-- page: 4 -->

Finding 1: the compressed air system operates at a higher pressure than the tools require and the leak rate measured during the audit was high. Reducing pressure and repairing leaks is the most significant opportunity identified.

Finding 2: the paint shop ovens run through weekends at standby temperature. Scheduling ovens to shut down during non production periods is recommended.

Finding 3: meter data for the main electrical incomer showed gaps, which limits the accuracy of monthly reporting. A data quality review of the metering system is recommended.

# Recommendations status

<!-- page: 6 -->

The site accepted all findings. Compressed air leak repairs are scheduled for completion within the next two quarters. The oven scheduling change requires approval from the paint shop quality lead.
