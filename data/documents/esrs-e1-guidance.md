# Purpose

<!-- page: 1 -->

This internal guidance summarises what the group must disclose under the European Sustainability Reporting Standard E1 on climate change, which applies to the group through the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD). It is a working summary for site teams and does not replace the standard.

# Greenhouse gas emissions disclosure

<!-- page: 2 -->

The group must disclose gross Scope 1, Scope 2 and significant Scope 3 greenhouse gas emissions in tonnes of CO2 equivalent. Scope 2 emissions must be reported using both the location based and the market based method. Sites must submit monthly emissions by scope to the group ledger no later than 15 working days after month end.

Emission intensity per net revenue must be disclosed. Sites must also report production based intensity internally so that group performance can be explained.

# Energy consumption and mix

<!-- page: 3 -->

The group must disclose total energy consumption from fossil sources, nuclear sources and renewable sources in MWh, and the share of renewable energy. Sites must meter renewable electricity separately and retain supplier certificates for guarantees of origin for at least 10 years.

# Transition plan and targets

<!-- page: 4 -->

The group must disclose its transition plan for climate change mitigation, including GHG emission reduction targets for 2030 with a base year, and the decarbonisation levers used to reach them. Each site must maintain an approved abatement project list showing expected reductions and implementation dates, reviewed at least annually by the site sustainability owner.
