# Scope 2 accounting

<!-- page: 1 -->

Scope 2 covers emissions from purchased electricity, steam, heat and cooling. The group reports Scope 2 under both the location based method, using average grid emission factors, and the market based method, using contractual instruments.

# Renewable electricity claims

<!-- page: 2 -->

Renewable electricity may be reported as zero emission under the market based method only when backed by valid contractual instruments such as guarantees of origin, renewable energy certificates or power purchase agreements that meet the quality criteria. Sites must record the instrument, the volume in MWh and the reporting period for every claim.

# Data requirements

<!-- page: 3 -->

Sites must report monthly electricity consumption in MWh, split into renewable and non renewable supply, and the emission factor source used. Changes to emission factors must be approved by the group sustainability controller before they are applied.
