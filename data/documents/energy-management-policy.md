# Policy statement

<!-- page: 1 -->

The group manages energy as a controllable cost and the main driver of its Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions. Every manufacturing site must operate an energy management system aligned with ISO 50001.

# Site responsibilities

<!-- page: 2 -->

Each site must appoint an energy manager, identify its significant energy uses, and install sub metering for any single use above 10 percent of site consumption. Energy performance indicators must be reviewed monthly by site leadership.

Compressed air systems must be leak surveyed at least twice per year. Steam systems must have trap surveys at least annually.

# Anomaly handling

<!-- page: 3 -->

Any month in which site energy consumption or emissions deviates materially from the expected level must be investigated by the energy manager. The investigation must check meter data quality first, then operational causes, and must be closed with a documented root cause within one month.

# Renewable energy

<!-- page: 4 -->

Sites must assess on site renewable generation every two years. Procurement of renewable electricity through power purchase agreements is coordinated by group procurement. Energy attribute certificates may be used as an interim measure and must be disclosed separately from physical renewable supply.
